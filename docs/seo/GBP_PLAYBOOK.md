### Services

Keep service descriptions concise and separate. Do not claim background checks, hospital-grade products, healthcare compliance, emergency response or after-hours availability unless they are documented and consistently deliverable.

Suggested structure:

- **House cleaning:** One-time and recurring cleaning for kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas. Scope and frequency are confirmed in your quote.
- **Commercial cleaning:** Scope-based cleaning for offices and other business spaces. Share facility size, traffic, preferred hours and cleaning frequency.
- **Deep cleaning:** Detailed cleaning for buildup and overlooked areas such as agreed baseboards, fixtures, cabinet fronts and grout areas.
- **Move-in/move-out cleaning:** Cleaning for empty or transitioning homes, with appliance/cabinet interiors added only when requested.
- **Post-construction cleaning:** Fine-dust and surface cleaning after active work and heavy debris removal are complete.
- **Recurring janitorial service:** Ongoing cleaning based on an agreed checklist, service window and reporting contact.

## Photo checklist

- Use original, well-lit horizontal and vertical images.
- Remove customer names, mail, family photos, access codes, license plates and medical/private information.
- Avoid repeated stock images, text-heavy graphics and fake before/after comparisons.
- Ask for permission before publishing identifiable private-property interiors.
