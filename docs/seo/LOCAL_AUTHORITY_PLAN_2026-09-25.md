## Earned-link campaigns

1. **Property-manager checklist:** publish a concise “Utah office cleaning scope checklist” and offer it to local property managers, coworking spaces and facility partners as a useful resource—not as a link exchange.
2. **Partner acknowledgments:** after a real relationship exists, ask suppliers, contractors, real-estate professionals and moving partners to add PRMB to their recommended local resources page.
3. **Verified project case study:** with client permission, document the problem, scope, timing, result and a customer quote. Ask the client to reference the case study from its own news/vendor page when appropriate.
4. **Local expertise contribution:** offer a short, factual cleaning-preparation article to one chamber or local property-management newsletter, with named PRMB authorship and a canonical service link.
5. **Community involvement:** sponsor or volunteer for one genuinely relevant local event, then request the standard sponsor/vendor listing. Do not purchase a sponsorship solely for link equity.

## Quality gate

Reject an opportunity when it:

- sells hundreds of directory links or promises rankings;
- publishes duplicate business pages without review;
- requires keyword-stuffed business names;
- hides pricing/renewal terms;
- has no real Utah audience or indexed directory;
- asks for reciprocal links as the main condition;
- would expose PRMB's private service-area address.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- citation claimed/verified/pending status;
- exact name, phone, URL and service-area consistency;
- referring domains and linked landing pages in Search Console;
- calls, forms and messages attributed to each profile;
- branded impressions for `PRMB Cleaning` and variants;
- referral leads, not just backlink count.
