## Draft structure after approval

1. **Situation:** service type, city and the factual problem.
2. **Scope:** what PRMB agreed to clean, plus material exclusions.
3. **Execution:** scheduling or access details that demonstrate real experience.
4. **Result:** observable outcome, supported by evidence.
5. **Customer voice:** the exact approved quote, if any.
6. **Service path:** one relevant service-page link and the canonical phone `(385) 314-9098`.

## Final review

- [ ] Facts checked by the named PRMB reviewer.
- [ ] Permission still covers every public detail and image.
- [ ] Copy does not reveal a private address, access code, security detail or personal information.
- [ ] Title and URL describe the service; they do not overstate the result.
- [ ] Structured data, if used, matches visible copy and does not invent ratings.
- [ ] Owner gave action-time approval before publication.
