A move clean is more than routine upkeep

Once furniture and belongings are removed, previously hidden surfaces become visible. The scope should account for an empty-space reset and the standards of the owner, tenant, buyer or property manager.

Kitchen transition

  • Accessible counters and sink
  • Exterior appliance cleaning
  • Cabinet fronts and agreed interiors
  • Floor edges and open spaces

Bathrooms & rooms

  • Fixtures and visible buildup
  • Reachable dust and touch points
  • Baseboards when included
  • Vacuuming or mopping

Final details

  • Property should be free of trash and belongings
  • Utilities and lighting should remain available
  • Keys, parking and access confirmed
  • Deadline coordinated with other vendors

Move-out cleaning

The goal is to leave the property ready for inspection, turnover or the next occupant based on the agreed checklist. Cleaning can support a good handoff, but it cannot guarantee return of a security deposit because landlords may evaluate damage, lease terms and other conditions.

Move-in cleaning

A move-in service focuses on starting with clean accessible surfaces before boxes and furniture limit access. Schedule enough time between previous occupancy, repairs and your move date.

Plan the timing carefully

  1. Finish moving and repairs first.Dust and traffic from movers or contractors can undo cleaning work.
  2. Confirm what remains.Identify furniture, trash, appliances and belongings that will still be present.
  3. List inspection priorities.Share a landlord or property-manager checklist when available.
  4. Protect the schedule.Provide a realistic access window before walkthrough, closing or key return.

Move cleaning questions

Does the property need to be empty?

Empty is best for access. If items remain, show or describe them before quoting so the scope and time are realistic.

Are inside cabinets and appliances included?

Only when named in the quote. These tasks add time and may require the appliances to be empty, cool and accessible.

Will cleaning guarantee my deposit?

No. We can complete the agreed cleaning scope, but the property owner controls deposit decisions and may consider damage or lease obligations beyond cleaning.

Can you clean while movers or contractors are working?

It is usually more effective to clean after they finish. If schedules overlap, explain the situation so we can determine whether a phased visit is practical.
