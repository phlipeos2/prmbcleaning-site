A practical scope for everyday living

A standard house cleaning focuses on the regularly used surfaces that affect how the home looks and feels. The final checklist depends on the quote, but these are common discussion points.

Kitchen

  • Accessible counters and sink
  • Exterior appliance surfaces
  • Cabinet fronts when included
  • Floor vacuuming or mopping

Bathrooms

  • Sink, vanity and fixtures
  • Toilet and shower surfaces
  • Mirrors and reachable details
  • Floor cleaning

Bedrooms & living areas

  • Accessible dusting
  • Vacuuming and floor care
  • General surface wiping
  • Priority areas named in the quote

Inside appliances, interior windows, heavy buildup, high-detail work and other add-ons should be requested before the visit so they can be priced and scheduled correctly.

One-time or recurring?

One-time cleaning fits a specific occasion, seasonal reset or trial visit. Recurring cleaning supports ongoing upkeep on an agreed frequency. A home with significant buildup may need a deeper first visit before a maintenance routine makes sense.

What affects the quote

  • Square footage and number of rooms
  • Current condition and last professional clean
  • Pets, occupants and access
  • Standard versus detailed scope
  • Frequency and preferred timing
  • Special surfaces or requested add-ons

How to prepare

  1. Point out priorities.Tell us which rooms or results matter most.
  2. Clear personal clutter when possible.Accessible surfaces let the visit focus on cleaning rather than organizing.
  3. Share access and pet instructions.Explain parking, entry, alarms and how pets should be handled.
  4. Confirm add-ons in advance.Ask about inside appliances, interior windows or unusually detailed areas before scheduling.

House cleaning questions

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Access arrangements are confirmed before the visit. Never send entry details through a public review or social post.

Should I choose house cleaning or deep cleaning?

Choose house cleaning for routine upkeep. A deep cleaning is usually better for built-up grime, neglected detail areas or a first visit that needs more time.

Do you bring products and equipment?

PRMB Cleaning brings the products and equipment needed for the agreed scope. Tell us in advance about preferred products, allergies or sensitive surfaces.

How much does house cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on size, condition, location, scope and frequency. A custom quote avoids advertising a low starting price that does not match the actual home.
