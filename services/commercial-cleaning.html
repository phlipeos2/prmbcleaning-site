Commercial spaces need a site-specific plan

An office, retail space and professional facility do not have the same traffic, surfaces or operating constraints. We use your walkthrough or project details to shape an appropriate checklist.

Work areas

  • Accessible desks and shared surfaces
  • Floors and traffic paths
  • Entry and reception presentation
  • Named high-priority areas

Restrooms & break areas

  • Fixtures and touch surfaces
  • Counters, sinks and exterior appliances
  • Floors and visible buildup
  • Consumables only when agreed

Operational details

  • Access and alarm procedures
  • Cleaning windows and frequency
  • Storage and approved products
  • Issue reporting and point of contact

Projects we can discuss

PRMB Cleaning lists office, workplace, retail, restaurant, medical/dental, recurring janitorial, deep and post-construction cleaning. Facility-specific requirements must be disclosed and approved before booking.

Medical or dental cleaning is not the same as claiming clinical disinfection or regulatory compliance. We will only include specialized protocols that are explicitly verified and agreed.

What determines the quote

  • Square footage and layout
  • Business type and daily traffic
  • Number of restrooms and shared areas
  • Frequency and preferred service window
  • Flooring and specialty surfaces
  • Security, access and reporting needs

From inquiry to working scope

  1. Describe the facility.Share location, use, size, hours and problem areas.
  2. Walk through or document the site.Photos or a site visit help identify surfaces, traffic and access constraints.
  3. Agree on tasks and frequency.The quote separates recurring work, periodic detail work and any exclusions.
  4. Set communication expectations.Name an operational contact and a simple way to report priorities or issues.

Commercial cleaning questions

Can cleaning be scheduled around business hours?

Share your preferred service window. Availability is confirmed during quoting; no after-hours or weekend schedule should be assumed until it is agreed.

Do you provide recurring janitorial cleaning?

Recurring service is available for qualifying business spaces. Frequency and task lists are customized to the facility.

Can you clean a medical or dental office?

PRMB Cleaning lists medical and dental environments among the facility types it can discuss. Provide the exact cleaning protocol and facility requirements so we can confirm whether the scope is appropriate. We do not imply healthcare compliance without a documented, agreed protocol.

Do you supply consumables?

Restroom paper, liners, soap and other consumables are included only if stated in the quote. Tell us what the facility expects before service begins.
